Policy

  • Critical: emailed immediately. High: included in a periodic summary email (every {{ config.DIGEST_INTERVAL_MINUTES }} min).
  • At most one email per alert every {{ config.EMAIL_PER_ALERT_MINUTES }} minutes unless its severity rises.
  • At most {{ config.EMAIL_MAX_PER_HOUR }} emails per hour overall; extra alerts roll into the next summary.
  • Suppressed emails are recorded below. Emails contain alert facts only - never raw log content.
  • SMS: at most one per alert every {{ sms_info.per_alert }} minutes unless its severity rises, and at most {{ sms_info.per_hour }} per hour; extra alerts are recorded as suppressed (email still covers them).
  • Transport: {% if config.SMTP_HOST %}SMTP {{ config.SMTP_HOST }}:{{ config.SMTP_PORT }}{% else %}dev outbox (SMTP_HOST not set; .eml files written to instance/outbox/){% endif %}

IP address privacy

Full IP addresses are never sent to the LLM. Changes are audit-logged.
